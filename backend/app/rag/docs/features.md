# Apple for Business — Feature Matrix

## Starter (1-19 devices)
- Full Mac, iPhone, and iPad lineup available at standard pricing
- AppleCare+ for Business available per device (accidental damage, priority repair)
- Apple Business Manager (ABM) account for centralized app/device purchasing
- Email support, next-business-day response

## Growth (20-99 devices)
- Everything in Starter
- Mobile Device Management (MDM) enrollment automation — new devices arrive pre-configured for the employee, zero-touch setup out of the box
- Dedicated onboarding specialist for the rollout
- Priority chat support, same-business-day response

## Enterprise (100+ devices)
- Everything in Growth
- Custom fleet pricing negotiated per device mix and contract term
- Trade-in program credits the existing PC/Android fleet toward the new order
- Dedicated Apple Business account manager
- Custom deployment SLA and phased-rollout planning

## Cross-tier notes
- Every Mac, iPhone, and iPad ships with a minimum of 5 years of security updates from date of purchase.
- AppleCare+ for Business covers accidental damage (deductible applies) and includes 24/7 priority phone support with Apple engineers.
- Apple Business Manager works with major MDM providers (Jamf, Kandji, Microsoft Intune) — IT doesn't have to switch tooling to adopt Apple devices.
- Data migration tools exist for both Windows-to-Mac and Android-to-iPhone transitions, run centrally by IT rather than device-by-device.
