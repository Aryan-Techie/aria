# Apple for Business FAQ

**Q: What's the contract term?**
A: No contract required for purchase — buy devices outright with volume pricing at 20+ units. Apple Financial Services also offers 24-month business leasing if you'd rather spread the cost.

**Q: What happens to our old devices?**
A: The trade-in program credits your existing PC or Android fleet toward the new order — trade-in value is assessed per device based on model and condition.

**Q: Can we use our existing MDM/IT tooling?**
A: Yes. Apple Business Manager integrates with Jamf, Kandji, and Microsoft Intune — IT doesn't need to adopt new device-management software to support Apple devices.

**Q: How fast can we roll out?**
A: With MDM enrollment automation (Growth tier and above), new devices arrive pre-configured for the employee — zero-touch setup, no IT visit required per device. Most rollouts of under 100 devices complete within 2-3 weeks of order.

**Q: What if a question comes up you can't answer on this call?**
A: Aria will say so directly rather than guessing, and can escalate to a human Apple Business specialist with full context from this conversation.

**Q: Is there a security/compliance review process?**
A: Yes — Enterprise-tier customers get a dedicated review and can request Apple's security documentation and compliance materials before signing.

**Q: Can we mix Mac, iPhone, and iPad in one order?**
A: Yes, volume pricing applies across the combined device count on a single order, not per product line separately.

**Q: Do employees keep their data if they leave the company?**
A: No — devices are enrolled under Apple Business Manager, which is IT-owned; access and data are managed centrally, separate from any personal Apple ID.
